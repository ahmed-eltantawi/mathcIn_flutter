Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

Welcome to SkillMatch. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by SkillMatch and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, use our job discovery features, or interact with our learning roadmaps, we may collect:

  • Personal details such as your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Career preferences, skills, project showcases, and CV details.
  • Device information, application settings, and usage analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our application services.
  • Personalize job recommendations and skill roadmap milestones.
  • Send you push notifications and relevant career alerts.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions or unauthorized access.

3. Data Protection and Security

We employ robust industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information and sensitive credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

SkillMatch may utilize trusted third-party service providers (such as notification delivery networks and cloud hosting services) strictly to assist us in operating our platform.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal account information at any time directly through the app settings or by reaching out to our support team.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our support team through the Contact Us section in the app.
